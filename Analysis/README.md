# Analysis

## Analytical Approach

The Karma's Choice 2022 Growth Plan analysis focused on using customer, employee, market research, and transactional information to answer the business questions provided in the case.

The analysis was completed using Microsoft Excel.

---

## Analysis Areas

### Employee Experience

Employee experience data was examined to understand whether employee engagement indicators could be associated with differences in customer spending.

This analysis was intended to help the executive team evaluate the potential business value of investing in employee engagement.

---

### Customer Retention

Retention trends were examined to understand changes in customer behavior and identify potential areas for improvement.

The analysis considered the relationship between retention and the company's broader customer experience strategy.

---

### Customer Experience

Customer experience data was analyzed to identify areas of the customer journey that could influence satisfaction, retention, and financial performance.

---

### Market Research

Market research data was used to provide a competitive perspective.

The analysis considered Karma's Choice's customer experience indicators in relation to external industry benchmarks.

---

### Revenue Growth

The findings were considered in relation to Karma's Choice's goal of achieving **16% revenue growth across locations in 2022**.

The purpose was not simply to describe the data, but to translate the findings into potential business actions.

---

## Excel Techniques

The project used:

* Pivot tables
* Pivot charts
* Data filtering
* Data aggregation
* Comparative analysis
* Business-focused data visualization

---

## Business Analytics Process

The project followed a general business analytics workflow:

```text
Business Problem
       ↓
Data Exploration
       ↓
Data Analysis
       ↓
Data Visualization
       ↓
Business Insights
       ↓
Strategic Recommendations
```

The final presentation translated the analytical findings into information intended for an executive audience.
