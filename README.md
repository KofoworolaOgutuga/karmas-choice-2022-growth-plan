# Karma's Choice 2022 Growth Plan

## Business Analytics Case Study

A business analytics case study developed for **Karma's Choice**, a specialty retail brand planning its 2022 business goals, objectives, and strategies.

The project analyzes customer experience, employee experience, market research, customer retention, and transactional data to identify opportunities for revenue growth.

---

## Project Overview

Karma's Choice set a goal of increasing revenue across its locations by **16% in 2022**.

The executive team wanted to better understand three key business questions:

1. **Employee Engagement and ROI**
   Would investing in employee engagement improve customer spending and provide a competitive advantage?

2. **Customer Retention and Store Investments**
   Retention decreased in 2019, while the company had recently invested in new store layouts and self-checkout counters. The analysis examined what the business should consider when planning for 2022.

3. **Customer Journey and Growth Opportunities**
   What opportunities exist to improve customer retention and support additional financial growth?

The project used Excel-based analysis, including pivot tables and pivot charts, to analyze the provided business data and develop recommendations for the executive team.

---

## Objectives

The primary objectives of the analysis were to:

* Analyze factors related to customer experience and spending
* Examine the relationship between employee experience and customer outcomes
* Investigate changes in customer retention
* Evaluate customer experience and market research indicators
* Identify opportunities to increase customer retention
* Identify potential strategies to support the 16% revenue-growth objective
* Present business insights in a clear, executive-friendly format

---

## Data

The assignment dataset contains three primary worksheets:

### CX — Customer Experience

Customer experience data collected through receipt-based customer surveys.

This data provides insight into customers' experiences with the retailer and can be used to identify relationships between customer satisfaction, retention, and spending.

### EX — Employee Experience

Employee experience data collected through email-based employee surveys.

This data was used to investigate employee engagement and its potential relationship with customer and financial outcomes.

### MX — Market Research

Market research data collected through external panel research.

The data provides a competitive view of the industry and helps place Karma's Choice's customer experience results within a broader market context.

---

## Data Measurement

The survey-based data uses a **5-point Likert scale**.

The market research data is competitive-based and is reported as percentages based on **top-box scores**.

Transactional data is reported in **USD ($)**.

## Key Business Questions

### 1. Does Employee Engagement Support Higher Customer Spend?

Karma's Choice was considering additional investment in employee engagement.

The analysis explored whether stronger employee experience indicators were associated with differences in customer spending.

The purpose was to help determine how employee engagement could potentially contribute to the company's broader revenue-growth strategy.

---

### 2. What Should Karma's Choice Consider Regarding Retention?

Customer retention decreased in 2019.

At the same time, Karma's Choice had made investments in:

* Store layout changes
* Self-checkout counters
* Customer experience improvements

The analysis examined customer experience and retention-related indicators to provide insight into what the company should consider for its 2022 strategy.

---

### 3. Where Are the Customer Journey Opportunities?

The project also examined customer experience data to identify potential opportunities throughout the customer journey.

The goal was to identify areas where improvements could contribute to:

* Higher retention
* Greater customer spending
* Improved customer experience
* Long-term revenue growth

---

## Business Goal

The central business objective was to support Karma's Choice's goal of achieving:

> **16% revenue growth across locations in 2022**

The analysis therefore focused on identifying customer and employee experience factors that could support sustainable financial growth.
