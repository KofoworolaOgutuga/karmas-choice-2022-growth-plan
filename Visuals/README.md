# Visuals

The visuals were developed in Microsoft Excel using the project dataset.

## Visualization Categories

Potential visualizations include:

* Customer experience comparisons
* Employee experience comparisons
* Customer retention trends
* Customer spending analysis
* Market research comparisons
* Revenue-related analysis

## Purpose

The visualizations were designed to communicate analytical findings clearly to an executive audience.

The goal was to make the relationships and trends within the data easier to interpret and connect them to the business questions presented in the case.

* Microsoft Excel
