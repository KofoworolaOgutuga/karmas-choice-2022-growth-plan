# Presentation

This folder contains the executive presentation created for the Karma's Choice 2022 Growth Plan case study.

## Presentation

**Karma's Choice 2022 Growth Plan**

The presentation was designed as a **15-minute executive presentation** for the leadership team of the simulated specialty retailer.

---

## Presentation Objective

The presentation communicates the results of the business analysis and addresses the company's 2022 planning priorities.

The presentation focuses on:

* Employee engagement
* Customer spending
* Customer retention
* Store investments
* Customer experience
* Customer journey opportunities
* Revenue growth

---

## Business Scenario

Karma's Choice established a goal of increasing revenue across locations by **16% in 2022**.

The executive team wanted data-driven insight into how customer experience, employee engagement, retention, and other business factors could contribute to this objective.

---

## Presentation Design

The presentation uses data visualizations and business-focused analysis to communicate findings to an executive audience.

The visuals were designed to make the analysis easier to understand and support the presentation of key findings.
