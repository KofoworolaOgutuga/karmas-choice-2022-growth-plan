# Data

This folder contains the dataset used for the Karma's Choice 2022 Growth Plan analysis.

## Dataset

**File:** `Data.xlsx`

The dataset contains three primary worksheets:

| Worksheet | Description                                                  |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **CX**    | Customer experience data from receipt-based customer surveys |
| **EX**    | Employee experience data from email-based employee surveys   |
| **MX**    | Market research data from external industry panel research   |

---

## CX — Customer Experience

The CX worksheet contains customer experience survey information collected through receipt-based surveys.

The data was used to investigate customer experience, spending, retention, and potential opportunities within the customer journey.

---

## EX — Employee Experience

The EX worksheet contains employee experience survey information collected through email-based surveys.

The analysis used this information to explore employee engagement and its potential relationship with customer spending and business performance.

---

## MX — Market Research

The MX worksheet contains competitive market research collected through external panel launches.

This information provides an external perspective on the retail industry and allows Karma's Choice's results to be considered alongside competitive market indicators.

---

## Measurement Scales

Survey variables use a **5-point Likert scale**.

The market research data is reported using competitive-based percentages based on **top-box scores**.

Transactional data is reported in **USD ($)**.

---

## Data Preparation

The original dataset was provided with the assignment.

The analysis was performed in Microsoft Excel using:

* Pivot tables
* Pivot charts
* Data comparisons
* Summary analysis

No original raw data values should be modified when reproducing the analysis.
